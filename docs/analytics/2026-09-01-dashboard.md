# Signal Forge analytics snapshot

Captured: 2026-09-01
Window: 2026-08-04 through 2026-08-31
Source: YouTube Studio channel analytics supplied by the channel owner

## Observed results

- 262 channel views and 0.3 hours watched in the 28-day window.
- The strongest technical uploads were `Microsoft Strikes Again` (75 views,
  6-second average view duration, 32.8% average viewed), `Server Prices are
  insane now` (60 views, 9 seconds, 43.8%), and `How are companies not using
  SPF/DKIM/DMARC?` (10 views, 13 seconds, 63.7%).
- In the live 48-hour panel, Microsoft led with 66 views; the airline story
  had 27 and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC story had 15.
- The dashboard sample is too small for a permanent lane decision. The clear
  first test is retention: stronger technical openings should deliver the
  concrete consequence before the source headline finishes unfolding.

## Next experiment

Use the `opening_and_pacing` treatment on one technical incident/security
story and keep one comparable source-headline-first control. The treatment
front-loads the short hook, then retains the exact source headline and the
complete source-backed narration. Compare average percentage viewed and
average view duration after the first 24-hour analytics check.

Do not infer success from raw views alone. Record impressions, CTR, average
percentage viewed, average view duration, likes, comments, and watch minutes
for each treatment.
