Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Clipping Tool is a private local application. The website hosting this policy does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

Data handled by the application

When enabled by its owner, the application uses Google OAuth to upload videos to the owner’s YouTube channel. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the owner’s computer and are not sent to the application publisher or stored by this website.

The application may also process locally configured media, captions, scripts, and platform metadata to create videos. These files remain under the owner’s control.

Third parties

Configured platform APIs, such as YouTube, process requests according to their own privacy policies and terms. Clipping Tool does not sell or use Google user data for advertising.

Contact

For questions about this private application, contact the repository owner through the project repository.
